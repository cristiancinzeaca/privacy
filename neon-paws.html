Neon Paws – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Neon Paws ("the game") is published by ElGatoLoco, Madrid, Spain. This policy explains what data the Android version of the game uses.

Data we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information. You do not need an account to play. Your progress (unlocked districts, achievements, settings) is saved only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. To show and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about your interactions with ads and the app;
  • diagnostic and performance data.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's pause menu (PRIVACY), where available. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

For details on how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
