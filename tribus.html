Tribus: Del fuego al cosmos – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Tribus ("the game", also published as "Tribes: From Fire to the Stars" and "Triburi: De la foc la stele") is published by ElGatoLoco, Madrid, Spain. This policy explains what data the Android version of the game uses.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store on servers or share any personal or device data. The game has no ads, no analytics, no user accounts and no in-app purchases, and it works offline.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, records, achievements and settings (language, sound) are saved only in your device's local storage. You can delete them at any time with "New game" or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
